# Taste log: Family Health Tracker (ACC 556)

Date: 2026-11-20

## Brief
- User: the Hansen family (about 8, all ages, phones), centered on Dad's heart transplant recovery; plus the ACC 556 class on a projector.
- Context: one-tap daily check-in, a quick look at the family board, a 5-minute live demo.
- Feel: calm premium (Oura / Apple Health). Light only. Total rethink allowed.
- References: none given.
- Must avoid: dark-first looks, harsh red or streak shaming, text or tap targets that are too small, paid-only fonts, the old navy/gray palette.

## Previewed (Today screen, design-candidates-today.html)
- A. Oura Ring (Refero): RECOMMENDED. Warm oatmeal page, serif headings, charcoal pill buttons, olive "done".
- B. Luffu (Refero): runner-up. Family-health brand, cream linen, deep teal ink, 6px square-ish shapes, no accent.
- C. Bevel (Refero): runner-up. Cool white with cloud-blue cards, Apple Health feel, Inter, capsule buttons.
- D. Alden (Refero): runner-up. White + parchment cards, sage pill buttons, sky-blue highlight on the title.

## Considered, not previewed
- Things (Refero): Apple-like cool gray + one blue; too close to Bevel.
- Function (Refero): cream + terracotta, strong, but rust reads like a warning next to check-ins.

## Rejected
- Aevi Wellness: white text on pale blue buttons fails contrast; 300-weight body text too thin.
- WHOOP, Eight Sleep, Impilo, Superpower: dark-first.
- Headspace, Tracky, Doo: too playful for the calm premium brief.
